About Muhammad Tayyab

Website & App Developer Helping Businesses Grow Online

I'm Muhammad Tayyab, a website and app developer based in Pakistan, working with businesses across Pakistan, the United Kingdom, and Australia. Over the past two years I've designed and shipped e-commerce stores, booking systems, and websites for clinics, restaurants, and retailers using React, Node.js, Firebase, MongoDB, and Flutter — always with the same goal: a fast, reliable product that helps you win more customers. I manage every stage of the project personally, from design through deployment and ongoing support, so you always know exactly who's building your site.
